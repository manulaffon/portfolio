Alexa Photo Frame

A smart photo frame for Amazon that looks and feels like a real picture frame — solid wood, a white mat, and the technology kept quietly out of sight.

Client

Amazon

Role

Industrial Design

Year

2026

Focus

Product Design · Materials

A Frame First

The Alexa Photo Frame was designed to sit in the home as a picture frame rather than a screen. A solid wood surround and a wide white mat give photos the presence of a printed image, so the product reads as part of the room instead of another device.

Details

The back is a dark, softly sculpted housing set into the wood. Mute and volume controls sit along the edge, the Alexa wordmark is debossed into the surface, and a fold-out stand lets the frame rest on a shelf or sideboard. The wood frame is finished with visible corner joinery, a detail borrowed from traditional framing.

Outcome

Alexa Photo Frame launched in October 2026.
