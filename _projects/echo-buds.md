    heading: Smaller, Lighter, Quieter
    body: |-
      The second-generation Echo Buds took everything learned from the first and reduced it. The earbuds are about 20% smaller and around two grams lighter, and the charging case is about 40% more compact — small enough to disappear into a pocket.

      Active noise cancellation replaced the first generation's noise reduction, blocking about twice as much background sound, with a passthrough mode a long press away for when you want to hear the world around you.

    heading: A Quieter Form
    body: |-
      The outer face became a single, seamless matte surface, replacing the first generation's glossy raised touch circle. The Amazon smile is printed tone-on-tone, so the brand is there when you look for it and invisible when you don't.

      A shorter nozzle and new pressure-relief vents make the buds more comfortable over long wear, and four sizes of ear tips plus two sizes of wing tips — checked with a fit test in the Alexa app — let each person find a secure, sealed fit.
